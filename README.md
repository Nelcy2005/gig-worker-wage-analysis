# Gig Worker Wage & Expense Analysis

Tableau-based visualization project of economic conditions of gig workers' community.

## Introduction
The current repository includes Tableau-based data visualization of economic difficulties facing delivery and ride-share drivers in the form of the .twbx file.

## Data
The underlying synthetic data is characterized by such metrics:
*   City and platform
*   Time spent & distance (km)
*   Deliveries made
*   Gross earnings and net income
*   Petrol expenditure and penalties
*   Strike day indicator
